How early exit normally works in the UAE (check your contract and your lawyer):

  • A tenant has no automatic right to leave early. The contract's early-termination clause, or a mutual agreement, decides.
  • Written notice is normally 60 to 90 days, and rent is payable until the notice period ends.
  • If the contract allows an early exit, the penalty is often 1 to 2 months of rent. This page suggests {{ penalty_months }} month(s): AED {{ suggested_penalty|floatformat:"2g" }}.
  • The security deposit can not be used to pay the penalty. It is refunded after the penalty and utilities are settled and the property is handed back in good condition.
  • Uncashed rent cheques for the time after the exit are returned to the tenant. This page cancels them for you.
